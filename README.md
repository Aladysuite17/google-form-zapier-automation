# Google Form + Zapier + Google Sheets Automation

## Overview
Automated lead capture and CRM system that streamlines client inquiries. When potential clients submit a contact form, their information automatically flows into a Google Sheet for tracking, and an email notification is sent for immediate follow-up.

## Features
- ✅ Automated form submission capture
- ✅ Real-time Google Sheets updates
- ✅ Instant email notifications
- ✅ Zero manual data entry
- ✅ Organized lead tracking
- ✅ Easy to customize

## Technologies Used
- Google Forms (form collection)
- Google Sheets (database/CRM)
- Zapier (automation/integration)
- Gmail (notifications)

## How It Works

### Step 1: Form Submission
Client fills out Google Form with:
- Name
- Email
- Business Type
- Inquiry Details

### Step 2: Automatic Data Flow (Zapier)
When form is submitted, Zapier automatically:
1. Captures all form data
2. Sends data to Google Sheets row
3. Triggers email notification

### Step 3: Instant Notification
Email alert sent immediately with:
- New inquiry details
- Client contact info
- Submission timestamp
- Direct link to Google Sheet

### Step 4: Organized Tracking
All leads collected in Google Sheet for:
- Follow-up management
- Lead analysis
- Client history
- Reporting

## The Result
**Saves 3-5 hours per week** of manual data entry and follow-up coordination. Zero leads fall through cracks.

## Setup Instructions

### Prerequisites
- Google Form
- Google Sheets
- Gmail account
- Zapier account (free tier works)

### Implementation Steps

1. **Create Google Form**
   - Set up form fields (Name, Email, Message, etc.)
   - Share form link with clients

2. **Create Google Sheet**
   - Add column headers matching form fields
   - Share sheet with team (if needed)

3. **Create Zapier Zap**
   - Trigger: Google Forms → New Response
   - Action 1: Google Sheets → Create Spreadsheet Row
   - Action 2: Gmail → Send Email
   - Map form fields to sheet columns
   - Set email template with form data
   - Test & activate

4. **Done!**
   - Leads now flow automatically
   - No manual work needed
